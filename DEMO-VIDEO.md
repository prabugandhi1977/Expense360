# Expense360 demo video — shooting script (~60 s)

## Setup

1. Open `index.html` with `?demo=overrun` on the end of the address, e.g.
   `file:///…/Expense360/index.html?demo=overrun`. This shrinks Engineering's
   budget (local mode only, never the shared workspace) so it is projected to
   overrun and the Reallocation Engine has a move to show. Clear site data to reset.
2. Use **Dark** theme, browser at 1920×1080, zoom 100%, bookmarks bar hidden.
3. Record with Win+Alt+R (Xbox Game Bar) or OBS. Do not sign in before recording.

## Scenes

| # | Time | On screen | Narration |
|---|------|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | 0–8 s | Landing page. Slow scroll from the hero to the four live tiles. | "Expense360 turns travel and expense spend into decisions. Here is the Budget Command Center." |
| 2 | 8–14 s | Click **Open Dashboard**. Type a name, role Finance, **Sign in**. | "Sign in by name. Finance sees the whole organization." |
| 3 | 14–26 s | Overview. Hover **Budget health**, then read the top **AI insight**. | "Budget consumed, time elapsed and projected year-end, side by side. Engineering is projected to overrun, and the insight names the category driving it." |
| 4 | 26–38 s | Click the Engineering row, then **Lodging**. | "Drill from Business Unit to category to the transactions behind the number." |
| 5 | 38–50 s | Sidebar **Reallocation**. Hover the suggested move, click **Approve move**. | "Idle budget in Sales can fund Engineering. One approval moves it, and the Audit Log records it." |
| 6 | 50–58 s | Sidebar **Finance Copilot**. Click "Which business is likely to exceed budget this quarter?" | "Ask in plain language and get answers from live data." |
| 7 | 58–62 s | Cut to the deck's closing slide. | "Expense360. Open the dashboard." |

## Notes

- Everything shown is seeded demo data; say "demo workspace" if numbers are quoted.
- The Copilot is a transparent keyword lookup, not a general AI chat; do not describe it as one.
- After scene 5, reload the page (with the same `?demo=overrun`) to retake from scene 3.
